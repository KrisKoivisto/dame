Why this one works

Celebrity apps have a graveyard

1 yrHow long Taylor Swift's app lasted. Thin content, and she stopped posting about it.

They didn't fail on demand. They ran out of things to show, and the star stopped showing up.

The assumptions
  • About 12 million people follow him across Instagram, Facebook and X, after overlap. 3% of them download the app in year one.
  • 12% are still using it a month later. Fan apps for one person should beat the 3–9% that entertainment apps average.
  • $300,000 in launch promo at about $3 per install, then 30% of that each year after.
  • Normal seasons. No deep playoff run built in.
  • 2% of monthly users buy the $29.99 Locker Pass. The presenting partner pays a $300,000 floor.
  • Apple and Google take 25% of in-app payments, blended.
  • Build, running and promo costs are US agency market rates. The full model shows every source and lets you change any of these.
